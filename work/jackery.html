Influencer Activations

The brief02 / 05

Jackery is a leading company in the portable power generation and power banks industry. Best known for providing reliable and original products for your on-the-go power needs.

The brief · 203 / 05

The foremost objectives of this campaign were to demonstrate the various use cases of Jackery’s products and to increase brand awareness among a targeted niche audience, but also for a broader audience too.

The execution04 / 05

Stride partnered with a mix of product review creators to effectively reach both niche audiences, where Jackery’s products are most aligned. A wider audience was also targeted to build overall brand awareness.

The execution · 205 / 05

The influencers created engaging and informative content showcasing real-life applications of Jackery’s portable power solutions, emphasising their versatility and reliability.

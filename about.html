About Stride.

One global group. Three businesses shaping how creators build careers, how brands reach audiences, and how the next generation gets started.

  1. 01Our story

    How it started.

    Alex and Ollie have been best friends since they were 12. Between playing sports, starting small e-commerce businesses and building themed Instagram pages, they discovered a shared entrepreneurial streak. School wasn’t easy for either of them: both have learning difficulties, and Alex dealt with dyspraxia and speech therapy.

  2. 022019

    Alex builds an audience

    Alex, known as Alxhendy on social media, grew his following to just under 6 million with pranks and comedy sketches made with his sister and friends. He signed with several talent management companies and found they fell short: they could do more for their talent's day-to-day lives, not just chase commercial deals.

    So Alex started helping his creator friends himself, with brand deals and content strategy.

  3. 032021

    The first logo and the start of Stride Social

    Alex took the idea of an influencer agency to Ollie, who was starting CMA Media, a paid ads agency, at the time. Together they decided to build it, with one mission: to simplify how brands work with influencers.

  4. 042021

    Our first influencer show

    Alex and Ollie at Stride's first influencer show in London: the Influencer Marketing Show.

  5. 052022

    Stride Talent opens

    Stride Social opens its influencer management arm, Stride Talent, with one goal: to make being a creator a better experience, down to getting talent into real-life events like film premieres and brand events.

  6. 062024

    The Influencer Marketing Show

    Stride Social, the advertising arm, has a clear vision: to connect influencers with fast-growing e-commerce and Amazon brands. In January 2024, sponsoring the Influencer Marketing Show in London marked a turning point. Standing beside such a high-profile event cemented Stride Social's reputation as a leader in influencer marketing: more than a sponsorship, it was a statement of intent to drive the industry forward.

    Stride also won an award from Hello Partners, which listed it among the world's top 30 influencer marketing companies to work with.

  7. 072024

    CES Las Vegas

    At CES in Las Vegas, Stride meets its overseas partners, Insta360 among them.

  8. 082024

    Over 200 talent

    Stride Talent grows to over 200 talent in less than a year and successfully enters the UK and US markets.

  9. 092024

    Stride's first Christmas

    At Christmas 2024, Stride sends its first gifts to its talent: parcels and cards, packed by hand at the office table.

  10. 102025

    New divisions

    Stride Talent officially launches divisions for each kind of content its talent publish: Gaming, AI, Family, Fashion, Technology, Beauty, Outdoors and Lifestyle.

  11. 112025

    Stride × SocialJet

    Stride partners with SocialJet to support growth in the UK market.

  12. 122025

    Stride Talent in Europe

    In November 2025, Stride Talent expands to Europe, opening its German and French departments.

  13. 132025

    In-house UGC production

    In November 2025, Stride launches its own in-house production for UGC content.

  14. 142026

    Stride Suite

    Stride launches Stride Suite, a dedicated platform where creators track their campaigns, payments and invoices, because we want full transparency for all our talent.

  15. 152026

    Holly heads Stride Talent

    In April 2026, Holly becomes Head of Stride Talent UK and US.

  16. 162026

    Ben heads Stride Sport

    In September 2026, Ben becomes Head of Stride Sport UK and US. Stride Sport officially launches and now manages over 150 athletes and sports creators across football, rugby, cricket, golf and fitness.

    Across Stride Group, Stride Talent reaches 700 signed athletes and creators, with 110 junior and senior talent managers.

  17. 172026

    Lisbon retreat

    In September 2026, the Stride team spends its retreat together in Lisbon.

  18. 182026

    Stride meets Youdao

    In October 2026, Stride meets the Youdao Ads team in China. Youdao (NYSE: DAO) is part of NetEase.

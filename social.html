Influencer Seeding

Influencer Seeding

Product gifting and creator activation at scale.

If you are looking for performance-based and cost effective influencer marketing , influencer seeding is one of the most powerful strategies available. This approach allows your brand to send products directly to carefully selected influencers who genuinely connect with your target audience. By gifting products instead of paying large upfront fees, you create authentic brand experiences that generate organic buzz, reviews, and user generated content. Our team handles the full process for you including campaign strategy, influencer selection, logistics, posting and reporting. This ensures your brand gets exposure through the right voices, while building a library of content that can be reused across paid ads, social channels and more. ‍

What's included

  • Creator sourcing and verification
  • Campaign strategy and seeding waves
  • Product logistics
  • Outreach and relationship management
  • Content management, ready to reuse in ads, email and social
  • Tracking and reporting

TikTok Shop

TikTok Shop

Affiliate creator campaigns and social commerce.

Our influencer affiliate programs transform one-off influencer campaigns into sustainable, revenue-driving growth channels. By converting top-performing influencers and loyal customers into affiliates, we build scalable systems that generate consistent sales while keeping costs low. Unlike traditional ad spend, this is a performance-based strategy where you only pay commission when a sale is made, delivering measurable ROI. Affiliate marketing with influencers also expands your reach into new, highly targeted audiences across TikTok, Instagram, YouTube and beyond. Affiliates create authentic user generated content (UGC) that builds trust and can be repurposed across your paid and organic marketing channels. With our full program management, recruitment and reward systems, your brand gains motivated advocates who consistently drive awareness, credibility and sales. ‍

What's included

  • Affiliate creator recruitment
  • Commission and reward structures
  • Full programme management
  • Creator content for paid and organic use
